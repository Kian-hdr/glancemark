# Glancemark Privacy Policy

Glancemark opens and edits Markdown files you choose on your Mac. It uses local storage for reading preferences and, when you choose Prepare Images for Finder, copies supported image data to an app group on your Mac so the Finder extension can display it.

Glancemark has no account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated document service. It does not send your documents to Kian Konrad.

If a document references online media, Glancemark may request that media over HTTPS. The media host receives the request and standard connection information, such as your IP address. The host handles that information under its own privacy policy. Links you open in your browser are handled by the browser and destination website.

If you request support, the details you choose to send are used to respond and diagnose the issue. Please remove sensitive material from examples and screenshots before sharing them.

For support or privacy questions, use [Glancemark Support](Support.md).
